Duel League

A league of one-on-one quiz duels. Opponents are paired Swiss style: the strongest available player meets the next strongest, and nobody plays the same opponent twice.

Tournament

Points per duel

Both wrong, or no answer in time, scores 0. Answering wrong while your opponent is right scores 0.

Ranking order: most points, then lowest total answer time, then most correct answers. A timeout counts as the full question time, and so does a bye.
