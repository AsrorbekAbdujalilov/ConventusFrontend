Land Rush

Answer quiz questions to claim hexagon tiles. Once the map is full, the fastest correct answers take land from the slowest players. Lose all your land and you are out.

Game

The map usually fills within 5 to 13 questions. With no question limit, playing until one player is left typically takes 60 to 230 questions, so a limit of 30 to 50 suits a class lesson.

How a question works

Claiming: while free tiles remain, every correct answer claims one tile, fastest first. When the map is full, anyone with no land is out.
Battle: correct answers rank first (fastest first), then everyone else. The top half of the active players each take one tile from the bottom half: 1st from last, 2nd from second last, and so on. With an odd number of players, the player in the middle neither gains nor loses. Only a correct answer can win a tile.
Winning: the last player with land wins. If a question limit is set, the player with the most tiles wins when it is reached.
